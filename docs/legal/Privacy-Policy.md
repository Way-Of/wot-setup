# WayOfTeams Desktop — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction

Way-Of ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how WayOfTeams Desktop ("Software") collects, uses, and protects information.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Local Data (On Your Device)

All of the following data stays on your device and is NOT transmitted to Way-Of unless you explicitly enable cloud sync:

- Source code and project files
- API keys and credentials (stored encrypted)
- Tickets, plans, and documents
- Agent configurations
- Memory and knowledge entries

### 2.2 Analytics Data (Optional)

If you opt in, we collect anonymized usage data:

- Feature usage frequency
- Error reports and crash logs
- Performance metrics
- Installation metadata (OS version, app version)

We do NOT collect: source code, file contents, API keys, or personal identifiable information.

### 2.3 Cloud Sync Data (Optional)

If you enable cloud sync, the following is stored on Way-Of servers:

- Your account information (email, name)
- Synced tickets, plans, and documents
- Team membership and roles

## 3. How We Use Data

- To provide and improve the Software
- To diagnose and fix technical issues
- To communicate about updates and security notices

## 4. Data Storage

- Local data: Stored in your application data directory
- Analytics: Stored in our analytics infrastructure
- Cloud sync: Stored in EU-based data centers

## 5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except:

- As required by law
- To protect our legal rights
- With your explicit consent

## 6. AI Provider Integration

When you use cloud AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic):

- Your prompts are sent directly to the provider
- We do not intercept or store your AI interactions
- Each provider has their own privacy policy

## 7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- Encryption at rest (AES-256) for stored credentials
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) for all network communication
- No backdoors or unauthorized access

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your data
- Delete your data
- Opt out of analytics
- Export your data
- Request correction of inaccurate data

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Software is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the Software or email.

## Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact: privacy@wayofteams.com
